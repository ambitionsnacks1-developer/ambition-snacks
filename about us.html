Who We Are

Welcome to Ambition Snack Product Limited, home of freshly popped, perfectly flavoured popcorn made with love.

At Ambition Snack Product Limited, we believe popcorn is more than just a snack — it is a crunchy moment of happiness. Our journey started as a small home-based project with one goal: to make popcorn that is irresistibly delicious, affordable, and enjoyable for everyone.

Every batch is prepared fresh using quality grain, clean ingredients, and creative flavours. Whether you need a quick snack, party treats, or popcorn for your shop, we are always ready to serve you.
